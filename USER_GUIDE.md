# User Guide — How to Use This App

A practical walkthrough of this Canadian personal finance app, organized around
real financial situations rather than just a feature list. Use it to find the
right tool for *your* money problem, whatever stage of life you're in.

---

## 1. Getting Started

1. **Register or log in** at `/login`.
2. **(Optional) Load a Demo Profile** — the fastest way to explore the app
   with realistic data instead of a blank slate. Go to **Settings → Demo
   Profiles**, pick a persona (see table below), choose 3–7 years of history,
   and click **Load this Profile**. This seeds accounts, transactions, debts,
   budgets, and investments so every screen has real numbers to show you.
3. **Add your real accounts** under **Accounts** (checking, savings, credit
   card, investment, etc.) — do this before entering transactions.
4. **Bring in your transactions** either manually or via **Import** (CSV
   upload from your bank — see §5).
5. **Start on the Dashboard** — it's your financial command center: Net
   Worth, Monthly Cash Flow, Savings Rate, spending by category, and budget
   alerts all in one place.

Press **Ctrl/Cmd + K** anywhere to open global search and jump to any page,
transaction, or account.

## 2. Which Features Matter for *Your* Situation

The app ships with 10 demo profiles spanning the full range of Canadian
financial life. Find the one closest to you (or blend a few) to know which
tools to prioritize first.

| Profile | Situation | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| 😰 The Overwhelmed Graduate | Paycheque-to-paycheque, maxed cards, student debt, no savings | **Budgets**, **Debts**, **Debt Optimization** (snowball for quick wins) |
| 💳 The Over-Leveraged | Spending more than earned, multiple cards + car loan | **Debt Optimization** (avalanche to cut interest), **Budgets**, **Bills** |
| 👩‍👧 The Single Parent | Mortgage + daycare, tight cash flow, slowly building savings | **Budgets**, **Bills**, **RESP Tracker**, **Goals** |
| 📈 The Young Professional | Disciplined saver, maxing TFSA, building RRSP | **TFSA Room Calc**, **RRSP vs TFSA**, **Investment Recommendations** |
| 🏠 The Average Canadian | Mortgage, RRSP, small CC balance — typical middle-class picture | **Net Worth**, **Budgets**, **RRSP Tracker**, **Properties** |
| ✅ The Comfortable Professional | No consumer debt, growing RRSP/TFSA | **Portfolio Rebalancing**, **Tax Planning**, **Financial Planning** |
| 💪 The Power Saver (DINK) | High earner, near-maxed TFSA, investing beyond registered accounts | **ETF Model Portfolios**, **Tax Planning**, **Investment Performance** |
| 🏢 The Business Owner | Variable income, large RRSP, commercial mortgage | **Tax Planning**, **Income Types**, **Paycheck Calculator** |
| 🌅 The Pre-Retiree | Large RRSP, mortgage nearly gone, counting down to retirement | **Retirement Projector**, **RRSP vs TFSA**, **Insurance Planning** |
| 💎 High Net Worth Executive | Multi-million portfolio, near mortgage payoff | **Investment Performance**, **Tax Planning**, **Annual Review** |

No matter the profile, three habits apply to everyone: **track every
transaction, keep a budget per category, and check the Dashboard weekly.**

## 3. Everyday Money Management (Cash Flow)

- **Accounts** — set up each bank account, card, or investment account you
  hold; balances roll up into Net Worth automatically.
- **Transactions** — log income, expenses, and transfers; merchant names are
  auto-categorized (groceries, restaurants, gas, utilities, etc.).
- **Budgets** — set weekly/monthly/yearly limits per category and watch
  actual-vs-budget on the Dashboard, with alerts when you overspend.
- **Bills** and **Recurring** — track due dates for recurring expenses so
  nothing is missed.
- **Spending Heatmap** — see *when* in the month/year you spend most.
- **Paycheck Calculator** — estimate take-home pay after Canadian taxes and
  deductions.

## 4. Getting Out of Debt

- **Debts** — list every balance, interest rate, and minimum payment.
- **Debt Optimization** — compare **Avalanche** (highest interest first,
  saves the most money) vs **Snowball** (smallest balance first, fastest
  psychological wins) and see your real payoff timeline.

## 5. Importing Bank Data

- Go to **Import**, download the CSV template, then upload your bank's
  export (CIBC, TD, etc. all work).
- Required columns: `date`, `description`, `amount` (negative = expense,
  positive = income) — or separate `debit`/`credit` columns.
- Add a `category` column to skip auto-categorization, or leave it out and
  let the app guess.
- **Bank Connections** lets you link accounts directly instead of manual CSV
  uploads, where supported.

## 6. Goals & Net Worth

- **Goals** — define savings targets (emergency fund, vacation, down
  payment) and track progress.
- **Net Worth** — assets minus liabilities over time, the single best
  long-term health metric.
- **Annual Review** — a year-in-review summary of income, spending, saving,
  and net worth change.

## 7. Investing & Canadian Registered Accounts

- **RRSP vs TFSA** — side-by-side comparison to decide where your next
  dollar should go.
- **TFSA Room Calc** / **TFSA Tracker** and **RRSP Tracker** — track
  contribution room and growth so you never over-contribute.
- **FHSA Tracker** (first home savings) and **RESP Tracker** (education
  savings) — for home buying and kids' education goals.
- **GIC Tracker** — track guaranteed investment certificates and maturity
  dates.
- **Investment Recommendations**, **Portfolio Rebalancing**, **ETF Model
  Portfolios**, **Investment Performance** — guidance on what to hold, when
  to rebalance, and how your portfolio is actually performing.
- **Properties** — track real estate as part of net worth.

## 8. Tax, Insurance & Retirement Planning

- **Tax Planning** — Canadian tax strategy and optimization ideas.
- **Income Types** — how different Canadian income sources (employment,
  self-employment, investment, rental) are taxed differently.
- **Insurance Planning** — life/disability/critical illness coverage needs.
- **Retirement Projector** — model when you can retire and on what income.
- **Financial Planning** — longer-horizon planning across goals.
- **Inflation Tracker** — see how inflation erodes purchasing power over
  time, useful when projecting future needs.

## 9. AI Insights & Reports

- **ML Insights** — machine-learning-driven spending pattern detection and
  anomaly alerts (richer with more transaction history — try 5–7 years of
  demo data).
- **Reports & Export** — generate and export financial reports.
- **Notifications** — alerts for budget overages, bills due, and goal
  milestones.

---

## 10. Tips

- Demo data can be regenerated or cleared anytime from the **DEMO MODE** bar
  in the header (visible once a profile is loaded).
- Settings lets you adjust currency/display preferences and manage your
  account.
- All registered-account tools (TFSA/RRSP/FHSA/RESP) use Canadian rules —
  if you're outside Canada, treat them as illustrative only.
